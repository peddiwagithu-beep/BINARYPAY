About HAKIM GLOBAL PAY

HAKIM GLOBAL PAY is an independent payment technology platform. Merchants collect M-PESA and bank-transfer payments, track every transaction and manage settlement from one dashboard.

What we do

We run the software: checkout flows, verification, reconciliation, reporting and API access. Payments are processed through Safaricom Daraja and approved banking and card partners.

What we are not

HAKIM GLOBAL PAY is not a bank and is not presented as a licensed payment service provider. We do not hold customer funds unless the required authorisations and settlement arrangements are in place.
